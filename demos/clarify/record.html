DISCHARGE SUMMARY

Diagnosis: acute bronchitis with mild dehydration.

Medications: Amoxicillin 500mg PO TID for 7 days. Guaifenesin 600mg q12h PRN. Albuterol HFA, 2 puffs q4–6h PRN for bronchospasm.

Follow-up: PCP in 5–7 business days. Rest for 48–72 hours and drink at least 2L of fluids daily.

Return to urgent care for: fever above 39°C, dyspnea at rest, or hemoptysis.

Plain-English summary

This is a discharge note for bronchitis and mild dehydration. It lists three medicines, asks the patient to rest and drink more water, and schedules a primary-care follow-up within one week.

PO TID

Take the medicine by mouth three times a day.

PRN

Use only when needed.

Dyspnea

Shortness of breath or difficulty breathing.

Seek urgent medical care for trouble breathing while resting, coughing up blood, or a fever above 39°C.

Personal action plan

Amoxicillin

500mg by mouth, three times daily for 7 days. Finish the full course.

Guaifenesin

600mg every 12 hours as needed. Drink water with it.

Albuterol inhaler

Two puffs every 4–6 hours as needed for chest tightness.

Follow-up

Book a primary-care appointment within 5–7 business days.

NOW

48–72 HOURS

DAY 7
